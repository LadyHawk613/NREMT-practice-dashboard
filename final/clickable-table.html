Student Instructions

The updated National Registry (NREMT) exam features Technology-Enhanced Items (TEIs), including Option/Check Box Table formats. In these items:

  • You must classify actions or findings by selecting the single most appropriate column category for every single row.
  • The Submit button remains disabled until every row has been addressed.
  • Several distractors are intentionally plausible actions; you must evaluate what belongs where given the clinical situation.

This interactive practice consists of 5 progressive TEI challenges covering Scene Size-Up, Primary Assessment, Secondary Assessment, Treatment & Transport, and Operations.
