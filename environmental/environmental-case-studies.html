NREMT National Education Standards Summary

Environmental Emergencies Core Rules

Key clinical indicators, priority interventions, and major contraindications for high-yield exam scenarios.

Condition Key Clinical Indicators Priority Interventions Major Contraindications
Heat Stroke Altered Mental Status + Hot/Dry or Sweating skin Rapid active cooling (ice to neck/axilla/groin, mist & fan), transport Oral fluids (aspiration risk), delay in cooling
Severe Hypothermia Core Temp < 90°F (32°C), Loss of shivering, Bradycardia Gentle handling, passive & active trunk rewarming, warm O₂ Rough handling (risk of V-Fib), rubbing frostbite, rewarming feet/hands first
Submersion / Near-Drowning Dyspnea, hypoxia, pink frothy sputum, atelectasis Airway suctioning, high-flow O₂ / CPAP / BVM, spinal SMR if dive involved Abdominal thrusts to remove water, delaying transport
Snake Bite / Envenomation Puncture marks, progressive edema, systemic signs Keep patient calm, immobilize limb at heart level, rapid transport Tourniquets, ice, cutting, or suctioning venom
Lightning Strike Unconscious, respiratory/cardiac arrest, cutaneous burns Reverse Triage (treat cardiac/respiratory arrest patients FIRST) Leaving crew in unsafe open locations
