What is the purpose of the primary assessment?
The purpose of the primary assessment is to quickly identify and treat immediate life threats.

NREMT Focus:
Airway → Breathing → Circulation
When should an EMT perform a rapid trauma assessment?
A rapid trauma assessment is performed when the patient has a significant mechanism of injury or suspected serious trauma.

Purpose:
Quickly identify hidden life-threatening injuries.
What does the "E" in SAMPLE represent?
E = Events

The events leading up to the illness or injury.

Example:
"What happened before you called 911?"
How often should an unstable patient be reassessed?
An unstable patient should generally be reassessed every 5 minutes.

Reassess:
• Airway
• Breathing
• Circulation
• Vital signs
• Treatment effectiveness
What comes before obtaining a detailed history?
The EMT must first complete the scene size-up and primary assessment.

NREMT Priority:
Identify and treat life threats before collecting detailed information.
